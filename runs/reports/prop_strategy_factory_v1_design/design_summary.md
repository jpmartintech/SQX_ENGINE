# PROP STRATEGY FACTORY V1 — Design summary

The Capacity Final Test showed that exact marginal portfolio construction improved the observed 5D maximum from the previous 2.316% to 4.066%, but the untouched validation block still produced 0% Challenge and Verification PASS and 0% coverage at 5% or above. The specialized line is therefore justified as a raw-material production experiment, not as a change to the validated General Factory.

PROP_V1 will manufacture portfolio components with explicit short-horizon vectors, independent timing, duration, cost, and bounded marginal utility. It will retain standalone quality floors and automatically emit EconomicSpec, R-ledger, ExecutionProfile, lineage, and PROP_READY metadata. The first implementation campaign is an A/B comparison against the General Factory using identical downstream evaluation.

No factory, strategy, grammar, MQL5, or MT5 logic was implemented or modified in this design phase.
