# Round 2E comparison method

`python_expected_predicate_trace_mt5_feed.csv` is generated by the frozen Python V1.7 feature/predicate implementation over the exact MT5 H1 export. The MQL5 trace must be generated with `InpDiagnosticTrace=true` and the same January tester configuration.

Join keys are `(strategy_id, signal_bar_time, predicate_n)`. Boolean results are the primary invariant. Numeric differences are reported as absolute/relative deltas and distance to threshold. Raw signals join on `(strategy_id, signal_bar_time, direction)`. Admission comparisons join on `(strategy_id, signal_bar_time)` and classify position/risk/concurrency separately.

No feed classification is permitted in this same-input comparison. Allowed classifications are `INDICATOR_VALUE`, `PREDICATE_BOOLEAN`, `RAW_SIGNAL`, `POSITION_STATE`, `RISK_GATING`, `CONCURRENT_ORDERING`, `ENTRY_ADMISSION`, `EXECUTION`, `DOWNSTREAM_STATE_DIVERGENCE`, and `UNRESOLVED`.
