# Good Strategy Contract V1

Hard gates: causal PRICE_ONLY definition, reproducible hash, evaluator replay success, valid costs, no DEV/VAL ruin, bounded 1% standalone heat.

Economic gates: DEV and VAL each require PF > 1, positive net return, positive exact economic expectancy, DEV trades >= 30, VAL trades >= 10. This is PASS/FAIL, not a ranking leaderboard. OOS is excluded from admission.
