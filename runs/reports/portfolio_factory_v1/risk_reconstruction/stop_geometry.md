# Stop geometry

`EXPLICIT_STOP`: all 12,289 stored definitions have positive `stop_atr` and the frozen evaluator computes `stop_distance = ATR(signal_bar) × stop_atr`. The sampled 200 representatives produced 13,861 reconstructed trades; each has entry/exit/initial-stop prices, gross_R, net_R and cost_R. No `NO_DEFINED_STOP` strategies were found in the Library.

A stop hit is approximately gross_R = -1 before costs. Gaps and execution priority can create small deviations. Net_R is lower because costs are expressed in the same price-unit model.
